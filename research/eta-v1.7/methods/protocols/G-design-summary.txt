Historical normal trajectories only. Select the first four completed pilot
trajectories in original cohort order (8101,8102,8103,8105) for calibration and
the first eight validation trajectories (8109..8116) for evaluation. Interrupted
8104 remains in the historical inventory. No selection by prediction accuracy.
These source numbers already appear in the manuscript; public data use matched

First actually readable executed result, P60 and P180; each anchor requires
the source final reply to occur later. Do not replace ended/missing slots.
Two independent repeats of Activity and canonical +Content at each anchor.
144 maximum new ETA requests; zero new solver starts. Failed started requests
are not rerun. GPT-6 Sol/medium, standard/default tier, subscription, one ETA
at a time, fresh released worker/session for each request.

Block order: deterministic SHA256 sort of seed 20261024, source identity and
anchor, separately within calibration and evaluation. Opposite condition order
across repeats; alternating first condition across blocks. Finish calibration,
freeze its coefficients, then dispatch evaluation. G-call-inventory records the
actual ordinal, stage, condition, anchor and repeat for all requests.

Fit a separate positive scalar per arm and anchor using the four calibration
repositories: minimize equal-task mean per-call absolute error, implemented as
a prediction-weighted median of truth/prediction with repeat weights. No test
prediction or outcome is used for this fit. Frozen values are published in the
calibration register. This describes the historical design, not a new fit.

Original main comparison: calibrated +Content minus calibrated Activity
task-equal MAE at P180 and its change from first result. Also report raw/raw,
all anchors, MARE for remaining>=60, task-mean Pearson, repeats, leave-one-repo-out
and 10,000 repository bootstrap draws with seed 20261024. Separately propagate
training and evaluation uncertainty where feasible. Repeats/stages are dependent.
Intervals are descriptive, outcome-aware and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.

Result: all 144 requests complete and settle with known usage. No solver starts.
Original calibrated P180 difference is -2.1 seconds with conditional interval
[-149.4,163.9]; it is inconclusive. Its joint training/evaluation interval also
crosses zero. Raw later-anchor gains are reported descriptively in the paper.
The raw presentation was selected after results were inspected; it is not the
original primary analysis. No equivalence or universal +Content benefit follows.
